# Jason's Revenge

## ▶ [PLAY](https://rockstarb0i.github.io/Jasons-Revenge/)

A VR horror game for Meta Quest 2 / 3. You are Jason Voorhees. It's Friday the 13th at Camp Crystal Lake, and the counselors are back.

Open the PLAY link in the Quest browser and press **Enter VR**.

- Left stick walks (click it to run), right stick turns (click it to sense where they are)
- Grip once to take a weapon, grip again to put it away; hold grip to hold a person
- Trigger squeezes: crush a skull, choke a throat
- B or Y pauses

Cut the phone line, wreck their car and kill the power — or someone calls the police.
